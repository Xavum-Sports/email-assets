Xavum email signatures

  1. Click Copy signature under your name.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings, then See all settings, then the General tab, and scroll to Signature.
  3. Click Create new, name it Xavum, and paste into the box.
  4. Pick it under Signature defaults for new emails and replies, then click Save Changes at the bottom.
